Understanding Tooth Loss Impact

Missing a Tooth?

A missing tooth affects more than just your smile layout. Here are common everyday challenges patients experience:

Difficulty Chewing

Struggling to enjoy firm, nutritious foods comfortably.

Difficulty Speaking

Mild slurring or phonetic changes due to the tooth gap.

Changes in Appearance

Gap visible during smiles and natural facial expressions.

Reduced Confidence

Feeling self-conscious in social and professional settings.

The Modern Solution: Dental Implants

Dental implants act as fixed artificial tooth roots that look, feel, and function similarly to natural teeth — providing a stable solution without altering adjacent teeth.

How Dental Implant Treatment Works

A clear, structured 5-phase approach ensuring predictable clinical care and aesthetic comfort.

01

Consultation & Clinical Assessment

Detailed examination of your missing tooth area, oral health assessment, and discussion of your medical history.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, medically responsible answers regarding dental implant cost, offers, and procedures in Hyderabad.

How much does a dental implant cost in Hyderabad?

At Shine Superspeciality Dental Clinic, Alkapuri, our current offer is ₹19,000 for the implant placement stage only, or ₹25,000 for the complete Implant + Crown package. Final cost depends on clinical evaluation.

Does the ₹25,000 offer include the crown?

Yes. The ₹25,000 Implant + Crown package includes the implant screw, the custom permanent crown, initial clinical assessment, and transparent pricing with no hidden fees.

What is included in the dental implant offer?

The complete solution includes a USFDA/CE approved implant screw, custom permanent crown placement, initial clinical assessment and consultation, and fully transparent pricing.

What is the difference between the ₹25,000 and ₹19,000 offers?

₹19,000 covers only the surgical implant placement stage. ₹25,000 covers the complete solution — implant placement plus your final custom crown restoration.

How long does dental implant treatment take?

Implant placement itself takes around 30–60 minutes per tooth. The full process — including healing/osseointegration and crown placement — typically spans 8–16 weeks, depending on individual healing.

Is dental implant treatment painful?

The procedure is performed under local anaesthesia and is generally comfortable. Most patients report only mild soreness for a day or two afterward, managed easily with routine care.

How long can a dental implant last?

With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, dental implants can last 15–25 years or longer, making them one of the most durable tooth replacement options available.

Do I need a bone graft?

Only if your CBCT/X-ray assessment shows insufficient jawbone height or width at the implant site. This is determined during your clinical evaluation and, if needed, is discussed and priced separately.

Can I replace a single missing tooth with an implant?

Yes — a single-tooth implant with crown is exactly what our ₹25,000 offer is designed for, restoring both function and appearance without touching adjacent healthy teeth.

Is everyone eligible for dental implants?

Most healthy adults are candidates, but eligibility depends on oral health, gum condition, bone density and overall systemic health. A clinical assessment at our Alkapuri branch confirms your individual suitability.
